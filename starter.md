Why Swords Bard over Lore or Valour? Swords Bard gets Blade Flourishes — specifically Slashing Flourish, which lets you hit two enemies with one attack using a Bardic Inspiration die. Combined with Divine Smite, that's smite damage spread across two targets in one swing.

The Band of the Mystic Scoundrel (Act 3 ring) is the item that takes this build to another level — it lets you cast Hold Person/Hold Monster as a bonus action after attacking, meaning you can attack, then set up the paralysis, then smite-crit next turn without burning your whole action.

Not much changes — Wood Elf is actually a solid pick for this build. Here's the honest breakdown:

What you keep that matters:

Darkvision
Fey Ancestry (can't be put to sleep, advantage vs charm)
Elven Weapon Training (longbow/shortsword/etc proficiency — won't matter much since Swords Bard covers weapons anyway)
Fleet of Foot — your movement speed bumps to 10.5m (35 ft). This is genuinely useful as a frontliner weaving between enemies.
Mask of the Wild — hide in natural terrain, niche but fun
What you miss vs the top picks:

No Savage Attacks (Half-Orc) — you lose the extra crit damage die. This hurts a little since the whole build revolves around guaranteed crits via Hold Person. Not devastating, but noticeable on nova rounds.
No Booming Blade cantrip (High Half-Elf) — minor loss, that cantrip is nice but not core.
Ability scores — Wood Elf gives +2 DEX and +1 WIS. That actually maps well: your DEX hits 17 with the +2 (starting from 15), which is exactly what the guide targets. The +1 WIS is wasted for this build but harmless.

Bottom line: Everything in the guide stays the same — same level order, same spells, same feats, same gear. Wood Elf doesn't break anything. You just won't have that extra crit damage die that makes Half-Orc feel so juicy on smite rounds. You'll compensate for it naturally as your CHA and gear scale up through Acts 2 and 3.
